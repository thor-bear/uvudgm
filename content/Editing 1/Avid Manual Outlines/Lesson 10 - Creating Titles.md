# Lesson 10 — Creating Titles

> [!abstract] Quick Glance
> Adding text to the sequence with **Avid Titler+**, the newest of Media Composer's three title tools. The key fact: Titler+ is a **segment effect** — it behaves like any other effect on the Timeline, applied either onto a clip or onto **filler on a higher video track**. Students create a title, fade it in/out, and edit it. This is the "put words on screen" lesson.

## Primary Keyboard Shortcuts

| Shortcut | Action |
|----------|--------|
| **V** | Splice-In |
| **B** | Overwrite |
| **Cmd+Y** | Create a new video track |
| **Cmd-click** a segment | Place the playhead on the segment's head (first) frame |

## The Three Titling Tools

| Tool | Use |
|------|-----|
| **Avid Title tool** | Classic, simple high-quality titles |
| **Avid Marquee** | Complex animated/3D title design |
| **Avid Titler+** | Newest; an effect-based tool working in all project types and rasters — **used in this lesson** |

## Titler+ Is a Segment Effect

This is the concept that organizes everything else: **Titler+ behaves like a segment effect.** That means you decide *what it sits on* before opening it:

- **On a segment** — applies the title to an entire clip (e.g. lower-third over a talking head).
- **On filler on a higher video track** — set IN/OUT on a new upper track over filler; the background plays on the lower track. This lets the title cover **part** of a shot or span several, and gives precise control over when it appears and disappears.

> [!tip] Build the track first, then title
> Create the new video track (**Cmd+Y**) and mark your IN/OUT over filler *before* opening Titler+. Students who open the title tool first get confused about where the title will land. Decide the "canvas" first.

## Creating & Editing a Title

Inside Titler+: click to place a text box, type, then reposition by dragging the frame and restyle (font, size, color) freely — exact placement doesn't matter while typing; refine after. Because it's an effect, you **edit an existing title** by re-entering Titler+ on that segment.

**Fades:** title fade in/out is handled like other effect fades, so a title can ease on and off rather than popping.

## Teaching Notes

> [!tip] "A title is just an effect on a track"
> If students hold one idea, make it this. Titler+ isn't a separate mode they navigate to — it's an effect living on a segment or on filler above the picture. Once that clicks, "where does my title go?" answers itself, and editing/fading titles becomes obvious because it's the same effect logic from Lessons 6–8.

> [!note] Filler-on-upper-track is the pro pattern
> Titling directly onto a clip is fine for a full-screen card, but the **filler on a higher video track** approach is how lower-thirds and partial-shot titles are really done. Teach both, but show why the upper-track method gives the control real work demands.

> [!tip] Restraint and legibility over flash
> Titler+ tempts students into elaborate styling. Steer toward the working editor's priorities: **readable, on long enough to read, safe from the frame edges, consistent across the piece.** A clean lower-third beats a fancy unreadable one every time.

> [!warning] Don't bury the title under the wrong track order
> If a title "disappears," it's almost always a track-order/selection issue — the title's track isn't above the picture, or the wrong track is active. Pre-empt it: the title track must sit *above* the footage it labels.

## Quick-Reference Answers (Review Questions)

- **The three Avid titling tools?** Avid Title tool, Avid Marquee, Avid Titler+.
- **What kind of object is Titler+?** A segment effect — it behaves like any other effect on the Timeline.
- **Two ways to place a title?** Onto a segment (whole clip), or onto filler on a higher video track (part of, or across, shots).
- **Create a new video track?** Cmd+Y.
- **How do you edit an existing title?** Re-enter Titler+ on that segment.
- **Why use filler on an upper track?** For precise control over when the title appears/disappears and to title part of a shot.
