# Electronic Press Kit (EPK)

## Definition
A packaged set of promotional materials — behind-the-scenes footage, cast/crew interviews, stills, synopsis — assembled once a film is finished, so journalists and press outlets can write about it without needing to conduct their own interviews.

## Teaching Analogies & Examples
- **A press release with homework already done for the journalist**: instead of a reporter having to track down interviews and stills themselves, the EPK hands them everything pre-packaged — the film's own "here's what you need to write about us" folder.
