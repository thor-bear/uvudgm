# Source Browser

## Definition
Avid's combined tool for linking and importing footage into a project — the main window used for what the industry calls "ingesting" and Avid calls "inputting" media.

## In Practice
Customized shortcut in this course: **Shift+I** (per the [[Avid Keybind Customizations]]). First used in Lesson 5 to bring Rock Climber footage into the project; students use the **LINK** button to link most of it while demoing an imported file too. Resurfaces in Lesson 6 as a fallback when drag-from-Finder linking fails on large footage sets (the 80+ file GNARLY B-Roll batch) — select the files with Link mode active and drag straight to a bin instead. Also supports a **Media Folder View** toggle for treating an entire offloaded camera card as one media volume rather than a maze of subfolders, relevant when students are handling raw camera-card structures directly.

## Teaching Analogies & Examples
- **The loading dock**: it's the one doorway everything passes through on its way into the building — whether footage is getting linked or imported, this is the window it comes through.
