# Splice-In

## Definition
An editing operation that inserts a clip at the playhead and pushes (ripples) everything after that point later in the timeline to make room — unlike [[Overwrite]], nothing gets replaced or lost.

## Teaching Analogies & Examples
- **The bookshelf version**: Splice-In shoves every book on the shelf over to make room for a new one — nothing gets removed, everything downstream just slides later to make space.
- **Squeezing into a line**: like a person cutting into a line of people waiting — everyone behind that point takes one step back, but nobody leaves the line.
