# Master Shot

## Definition
A wide shot capturing an entire scene's action and dialogue from a single, unbroken angle — the shot every other angle (OTS, medium, close-up) is measured against, and the one an editor typically starts assembling from before cutting into coverage.

## Teaching Analogies & Examples
- **The establishing wide shot of a photo shoot**: a photographer shoots the full scene first to see the whole setup before zooming in for detail shots — the Master is that wide reference everything else gets built around.
- The "why lighting drives it" reason is the one that surprises students most — most assume it's about performance or blocking, so leading with "actually, it's mostly about the lights" is a good way to make the question land as a genuine "guess why" rather than an obvious one.
