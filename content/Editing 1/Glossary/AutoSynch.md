# AutoSynch

## Definition
Avid's automatic audio/video sync tool — matches separately recorded production audio to camera footage (typically using timecode or a slate reference) and produces synced subclips ready to edit with.

## In Practice
Customized shortcut in this course: **Shift+G** (per the [[Avid Keybind Customizations]] — a mapping that overwrites an existing default binding, worth double-checking on lab machines). Used in Lesson 26 to bring the Fisker dialogue media into Avid and sync it — the Avid-side counterpart to Premiere's **Create Multi-Camera Source Sequence** workflow taught earlier in the same lesson. AutoSynch produces Subclips rather than a Processed Clips folder (Avid has no equivalent to that Premiere feature) — the course's workaround is simply color-coding clips as they're finished syncing.

## Teaching Analogies & Examples
- **Matching two puzzle pieces by their cut edge**: separate audio and video are like two halves of the same photo cut apart — AutoSynch finds the matching edge (usually the slate clap) and lines them back up automatically instead of you eyeballing it.
