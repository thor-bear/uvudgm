# Extend

## Definition
A trim operation that moves the nearest edit point to meet the current position of the playhead — a fast, mouse-free way to trim without entering full Trim Mode drag interactions.

## In Practice
Customized shortcut in Avid: **Shift+=** (per the [[Avid Keybind Customizations]]). Framed in Lesson 11 as a technique some editors consider superseded by JKL trimming, but still worth teaching — it's referenced often enough in industry interviews/podcasts that students should recognize it by name even if they don't reach for it daily. In Premiere it's called **Extend to Playhead**, shortcut **E** (Lesson 18), and gets a second, more embedded pass during the live Scene 7 demo in Lesson 19b, taught in the context of an actual edit rather than as an isolated feature.

## Teaching Analogies & Examples
- **"Stretch to meet me here"**: park your playhead where you want the edit to land, hit Extend, and the nearest edge of the clip stretches (or shrinks) to meet it — like pulling a curtain to exactly where you're standing instead of measuring the distance first.
- Useful contrast with dragging: dragging is eyeballing a trim with the mouse; Extend is saying "wherever my playhead currently is — that's the new edge," no aiming required.
