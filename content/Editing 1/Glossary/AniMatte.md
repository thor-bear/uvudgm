# AniMatte

## Definition
An Avid effect that builds an animated, shape-based matte over time — used to selectively cover or reveal part of a frame across a moving shot, rather than masking a single static frame.

## In Practice
Nicknamed "the Secret Weapon" in the Stranger Things Assistant Editor interview shown back in Lesson 15 — Lesson 27 deliberately calls that connection out loud as a callback rather than a coincidence. Demoed removing a boy who passes through the background at 16:33 of `R12_05 Jason and Sarah Interview`: copy V1's video onto V2, slip V2 so the background plate lines up differently than the foreground, then build a rough two-shape AniMatte to cover him. Students attempt the technique themselves — it's explicitly framed as something that doesn't need to be perfect, just conceptually understood. One of the lesson's four required turn-in screenshots (with both mattes selected).

## Teaching Analogies & Examples
- **A moving stencil**: a regular matte is a stencil you hold still over a picture; AniMatte is that same stencil, but it can shift shape and position over time to keep covering something that's moving — like tracing around a walking person frame by frame instead of drawing one fixed outline.
- The "Secret Weapon" nickname from the Stranger Things interview is worth repeating verbatim in class — it signals to students this is a real working-editor tool, not just a classroom exercise.
