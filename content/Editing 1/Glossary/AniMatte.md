# AniMatte

## Definition
An Avid effect that builds an animated, shape-based matte over time — used to selectively cover or reveal part of a frame across a moving shot, rather than masking a single static frame.

## Teaching Analogies & Examples
- **A moving stencil**: a regular matte is a stencil you hold still over a picture; AniMatte is that same stencil, but it can shift shape and position over time to keep covering something that's moving — like tracing around a walking person frame by frame instead of drawing one fixed outline.
