# Slide

## Definition
Moves a selected clip earlier or later in the sequence while automatically trimming its two neighbors to compensate — the clip's own in/out points and length stay fixed, but its position shifts and the surrounding edit points adjust to absorb the change.

## In Practice
Lesson 11 teaches Slide as a natural extension of the same session's alternate (non-Trim-Mode) method for [[Slip]] — the lesson explicitly revisits that Slip technique here specifically as a bridge into understanding Slide, rather than teaching the two as unrelated tools. Reinforced again in Lesson 19b's live Scene 7 edit alongside Slip, Extend to Playhead, and Top and Tail — all taught inside a real editorial decision rather than as an isolated feature list.

## Teaching Analogies & Examples
- **Sliding a picture frame along the wall**: this time the frame itself (the clip) moves left or right — but the photo inside it (what part of the source is showing) stays exactly the same; the neighbors on either side just stretch or shrink to absorb the move.
- Pairs directly with Slip as the mirror image: "Slip moves the photo inside a fixed frame; Slide moves the frame along the wall." Worth teaching them back-to-back specifically because the names alone don't make the distinction obvious.
