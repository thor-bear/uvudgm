# Slide

## Definition
Moves a selected clip earlier or later in the sequence while automatically trimming its two neighbors to compensate — the clip's own in/out points and length stay fixed, but its position shifts and the surrounding edit points adjust to absorb the change.

## Teaching Analogies & Examples
- **Sliding a picture frame along the wall**: this time the frame itself (the clip) moves left or right — but the photo inside it (what part of the source is showing) stays exactly the same; the neighbors on either side just stretch or shrink to absorb the move.
