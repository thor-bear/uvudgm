# Shooting Script

## Definition
The finalized version of a screenplay used on set during production — distinct from earlier drafts, formatted and numbered specifically to match how the film will actually be shot and scheduled.

## Teaching Analogies & Examples
- **The final blueprint, not the sketch**: earlier drafts of a script are like early sketches of a house — ideas still in flux. The Shooting Script is the locked blueprint the construction crew actually builds from, numbered and formatted to match exactly how the shoot will run.
