# Essential Graphics (Graphics Properties)

## Definition
Premiere's built-in title and graphics workflow — a Graphics Properties panel controlling text and shape attributes (font, size, leading, tracking, justification, fill, stroke, shadow) directly on a title layer placed over video.

## Teaching Analogies & Examples
- **A word processor's formatting toolbar, but for on-screen text**: font, size, color, alignment — the same categories of control students already know from writing a paper, just applied to text that sits over video instead of a page.
