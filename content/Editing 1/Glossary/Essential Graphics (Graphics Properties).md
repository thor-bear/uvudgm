# Essential Graphics (Graphics Properties)

## Definition
Premiere's built-in title and graphics workflow — a Graphics Properties panel controlling text and shape attributes (font, size, leading, tracking, justification, fill, stroke, shadow) directly on a title layer placed over video.

## In Practice
Introduced in Lesson 21 using a "Landscape for Graphics" still image: a title is created and every property is walked through in the Graphics Properties window, then positioned using an Eyedropper to sample the sky color for the fill — specifically chosen to make Shadow and Stroke visible against a matching background. The lesson explicitly flags this section as unfinished, still being developed by the colleague — treat the Graphics content here as a foundation to build on rather than a settled curriculum. Feeds directly into [[Keyframe Animation (Bezier Ease-In, Ease-Out)]], since the same title's Effects Controls panel is where its animation gets built.

## Teaching Analogies & Examples
- **A word processor's formatting toolbar, but for on-screen text**: font, size, color, alignment — the same categories of control students already know from writing a paper, just applied to text that sits over video instead of a page.
