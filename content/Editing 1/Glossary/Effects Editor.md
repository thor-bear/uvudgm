# Effects Editor

## Definition
Avid's dedicated panel for adjusting an effect's parameters in detail — the working space where an applied effect actually gets shaped, as opposed to the Effects Palette, which is just where effects are browsed and applied.

## In Practice
Menu shortcut in this course: **Shift+7** (Tools > Effects Editor, per the [[Avid Keybind Customizations]]). Lesson 27 also flags a separate, more specific gesture worth distinguishing from that menu shortcut: **always open the Effects Editor with Option held** when double-clicking an effect directly, rather than relying on the general menu path — the two aren't interchangeable habits, just two different ways into the same panel. Used throughout the lesson's Avid effects survey (PIP, nesting, AniMatte).

## Teaching Analogies & Examples
- **A workbench, not a store shelf**: the Effects Palette is where you browse and pick up an effect, like walking the aisles of a store; the Effects Editor is the workbench where you actually adjust it once it's in your hands.
