# Effects Editor

## Definition
Avid's dedicated panel for adjusting an effect's parameters in detail — the working space where an applied effect actually gets shaped, as opposed to the Effects Palette, which is just where effects are browsed and applied.

## Teaching Analogies & Examples
- **A workbench, not a store shelf**: the Effects Palette is where you browse and pick up an effect, like walking the aisles of a store; the Effects Editor is the workbench where you actually adjust it once it's in your hands.
