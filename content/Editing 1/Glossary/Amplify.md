# Amplify

## Definition
A Premiere audio effect that boosts a clip's level beyond what the standard gain/mixer controls comfortably allow — used when material was recorded cleanly but at too low a level to reach target loudness through normal adjustment alone.

## Teaching Analogies & Examples
- **A megaphone, not a repair kit**: Amplify makes clean-but-quiet audio louder — it doesn't fix audio that's actually damaged (distorted, noisy, clipped). If the recording itself has a problem, turning it up just makes the problem louder too.
