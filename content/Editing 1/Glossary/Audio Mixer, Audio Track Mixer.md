# Audio Mixer, Audio Track Mixer

## Definition
A dedicated panel showing every track's level as a channel strip, letting an editor ride Gain across multiple tracks at once with visual metering — the same underlying adjustment as riding gain directly in the timeline, just presented as a mixing-console-style interface.

## In Practice
In Avid, the Audio Mixer lives inside the Audio Workspace (Lesson 12) — students are explicitly reminded to switch back to their personal/Edit workspace afterward rather than staying parked there. In Premiere (Lesson 22), the equivalent is the **Audio Track Mixer**, described directly as functionally the same adjustment as riding gain in the timeline — the course draws this parallel deliberately so the Avid-trained half of the class doesn't have to relearn the underlying concept, just the interface.

## Teaching Analogies & Examples
- **A soundboard at a live concert**: every track gets its own physical fader, and the sound engineer can see and ride all of them at once — the Audio Mixer is that same board, just on screen instead of in a venue.
- Good reassurance for Avid-trained students moving to Premiere: "you already know how to do this, it's just wearing a different outfit" — same underlying skill (riding gain), different interface.
