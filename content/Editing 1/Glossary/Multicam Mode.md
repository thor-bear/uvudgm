# Multicam Mode

## Definition
An editing mode that displays multiple synced camera angles at once and lets an editor switch between them live, in real time, while the sequence records which angle was active at each moment.

## Teaching Analogies & Examples
- **A live TV control room**: a director sits in front of a wall of camera feeds and punches a button to switch which one goes to air, in real time, while the show is happening — Multicam Mode is that same live-switching experience, just for editing after the fact.
