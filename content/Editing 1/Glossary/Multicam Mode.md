# Multicam Mode

## Definition
An editing mode that displays multiple synced camera angles at once and lets an editor switch between them live, in real time, while the sequence records which angle was active at each moment.

## In Practice
Set up in Lesson 26 after building a **Create Multi-Camera Source Sequence** in Premiere: add the Multicam toolbar button, then edit and switch live between Camera A and B directly in the timeline. Demonstrated further with the **UNLEASHED** exercise — a multichannel sequence edited live using the number keys **1–0** to punch between angles, with students invited to try it themselves if time allows. The lesson notes Avid's multicam feature works identically in concept, just with its keys mapped separately.

## Teaching Analogies & Examples
- **A live TV control room**: a director sits in front of a wall of camera feeds and punches a button to switch which one goes to air, in real time, while the show is happening — Multicam Mode is that same live-switching experience, just for editing after the fact.
- The UNLEASHED exercise is worth framing explicitly as "you're the director now" — handing students the number keys and letting them punch between angles live gives them a felt sense of the tool that a lecture alone can't.
