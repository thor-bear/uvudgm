# Foley

## Definition
Custom-performed sound effects recorded to match on-screen action — footsteps, cloth movement, small physical contact sounds — recreated by a performer watching the picture, rather than pulled from a generic sound-effects library.

## Teaching Analogies & Examples
- **A one-person orchestra of household objects**: a Foley artist watches the screen and performs footsteps, cloth rustling, and impacts live with props — often nothing like the real object (a baseball bat hitting a leather jacket for a punch, a plastic sheet and poured water for rain on an umbrella).
