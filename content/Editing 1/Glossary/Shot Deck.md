# Shot Deck

## Definition
A curated collection of reference shots or images assembled to communicate a visual style, mood, or shooting plan before or during production — a shorthand visual pitch rather than a written description.

## Teaching Analogies & Examples
- **A mood board for a room renovation**: before buying a single piece of furniture, you'd pin images of the look you're going for — a Shot Deck is that same move applied to a film's visual style, gathered before a single frame is shot.
