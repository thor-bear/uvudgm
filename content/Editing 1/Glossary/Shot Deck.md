# Shot Deck

## Definition
A curated collection of reference shots or images assembled to communicate a visual style, mood, or shooting plan before or during production — a shorthand visual pitch rather than a written description.

## In Practice
Introduced as early as Day 1, where the Shotdeck.com assignment is framed as fun and creative — a way to get students recognizing what editing (and, more broadly, visual storytelling) is really about before any software is touched. Reviewed properly in Lesson 3, where the instructor pre-screens submissions to showcase good examples and has students with too many shots redo the assignment. Resurfaces in Lesson 16 as a professional-context lesson: a quick pass on what a shot deck actually is and why students will keep making them throughout their schooling and careers, tying the early creative exercise back to real industry practice.

## Teaching Analogies & Examples
- **A mood board for a room renovation**: before buying a single piece of furniture, you'd pin images of the look you're going for — a Shot Deck is that same move applied to a film's visual style, gathered before a single frame is shot.
