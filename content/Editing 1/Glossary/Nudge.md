# Nudge

## Definition
Moves a selected segment a small, precise number of frames earlier or later in the sequence, overwriting whatever it lands on — a fine-tuning tool for shifting a clip's timing without a full drag-and-drop move.

## In Practice
Taught in Lesson 11 immediately after Slip: select the segment, then use the Trim Tools to nudge it. A quieter, less-discussed tool in the outline compared to Slip and Slide, but grouped with them as part of the same "precision positioning" toolkit students build out in this lesson.

## Teaching Analogies & Examples
- **Bumping a picture frame on the wall**: a small, deliberate nudge left or right to fine-tune position — not a full rehang, just a tiny correction.
