# Pen Tool (Audio)

## Definition
Premiere's tool for drawing level changes directly onto a clip's audio waveform in the timeline — click to add a control point, drag to raise or lower level at that point — used to remove, isolate, or boost specific moments of audio without opening a separate mixer.

## Teaching Analogies & Examples
- **Drawing directly on the waveform like a graph**: instead of opening a separate mixer panel, you click straight onto the sound wave itself and drag a point up or down — like adjusting a line graph by grabbing one point on the line.
