# Quick Transition Tool

## Definition
Avid's fast way to apply a transition at an edit point — drop it directly onto a cut and either type an exact duration or adjust it manually within the tool, without opening a separate effects browser.

## In Practice
Shortcut: **\\**. Introduced in Lesson 7 right after the Cuts & Transitions 101 video, as the first hands-on transitions tool students touch. The lesson pairs it immediately with the **Transition Manipulation Tool** for adjusting transitions already sitting in the sequence, and the **six-window display**, which shows the start/middle/end of the transition on both sides of the cut — useful for understanding exactly what material a dissolve is drawing from. Directly connects to the [[Handles (Transition)]] discussion, since a transition literally can't be built without enough handle on both sides.

## Teaching Analogies & Examples
- **A dial you can type a number into**: instead of eyeballing a drag until it looks right, you can just type "12 frames" and get an exact, repeatable transition length every time.
