# JKL

## Definition
The three-key playback scheme used across nearly every professional NLE: **J** plays backward, **K** pauses (and acts as a modifier when held with J or L), **L** plays forward. Pressing J or L repeatedly increases playback speed in that direction.

## Teaching Analogies & Examples
- **Steering wheel and pedals, but for time**: J is reverse, L is forward, K is the brake — and just like a car, tapping J or L again adds more speed in that direction. K alone stops you dead on a frame.
- **It's a three-key volume knob for time travel**: holding L longer doesn't just keep playing, it makes you go faster forward; the same is true in reverse with J.
- If a student's hand cramps trying to use the mouse to scrub, remind them JKL is designed for exactly that frustration — it's built so an editor's eyes never have to leave the screen to find a button.
