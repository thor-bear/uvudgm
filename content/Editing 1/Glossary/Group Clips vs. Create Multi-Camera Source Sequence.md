# Group Clips vs. Create Multi-Camera Source Sequence

## Definition
Two different Premiere ways to combine multiple synced camera angles of the same moment. **Create Multi-Camera Source Sequence** links the angles together while keeping each one's own metadata intact. **Group Clips** merges them together permanently, destroying that metadata in the process.

## Teaching Analogies & Examples
- **A stapled stack of photos vs. a paperclip**: Group Clips staples the footage together permanently, destroying each individual photo's own label in the process. Create Multi-Camera Source Sequence is a paperclip — it holds them together just as usefully, but you can still pull one out and see its original label anytime.
