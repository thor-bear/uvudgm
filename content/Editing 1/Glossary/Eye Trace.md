# Eye Trace

## Definition
The idea that a viewer's eye lands on a predictable point of interest within a frame, and that a cut lands most smoothly when the next shot picks up close to where the eye already was — minimizing the visual "hunt" the audience has to do after every edit. Fourth on Walter Murch's Rule of Six, behind Emotion, Story, and Rhythm.
