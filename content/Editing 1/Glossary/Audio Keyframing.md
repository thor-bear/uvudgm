# Audio Keyframing

## Definition
Setting specific points in time where an audio level (typically Volume, not Gain) changes value, so the level ramps or steps between those points automatically rather than staying flat across a clip.

## In Practice
Introduced in Lesson 12 as the mechanism behind Volume adjustments specifically — Gain is a flat, one-time setting, but boosting an interview properly usually needs Volume shaping on top, which is where keyframes come in. Taught in the same breath as the Audio Mixer and the general Gain-vs-Volume distinction, positioned as the tool that makes Volume "modulated" rather than static.

## Teaching Analogies & Examples
- **Connect-the-dots for volume**: you drop a few dots (keyframes) at specific moments, and the software automatically draws the smooth line between them — you're not manually riding the level every single frame, just setting a few checkpoints.
