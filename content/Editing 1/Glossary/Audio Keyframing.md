# Audio Keyframing

## Definition
Setting specific points in time where an audio level (typically Volume, not Gain) changes value, so the level ramps or steps between those points automatically rather than staying flat across a clip.

## Teaching Analogies & Examples
- **Connect-the-dots for volume**: you drop a few dots (keyframes) at specific moments, and the software automatically draws the smooth line between them — you're not manually riding the level every single frame, just setting a few checkpoints.
