# Audio Ducking

## Definition
Automatically lowering the level of one audio track (typically music) whenever another track (typically dialogue) is present, so the two don't compete — then restoring the music level once the dialogue clears.

## Teaching Analogies & Examples
- **A restaurant automatically turning down music when you talk to the host**: the background sound dips whenever the foreground sound (dialogue) needs the spotlight, then comes back up once it's quiet again — automatically, without someone manually riding the fader.
