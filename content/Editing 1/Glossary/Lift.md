# Lift

## Definition
Removes a selected clip (or the material between In/Out points) from the sequence and leaves a gap behind — unlike [[Extract]], nothing shifts to fill the space.

## In Practice
Shortcut: **Z**. Introduced in Lesson 9 alongside Extract as the two core tools for building a **Radio Edit** from rough interview footage. Lift is the gentler of the pair — useful when you want to pull something out but aren't ready to commit to closing the gap yet (e.g. isolating a section for review without disturbing surrounding timing).

## Teaching Analogies & Examples
- **Pulling a book off the shelf and leaving the gap**: Lift takes something out but doesn't slide the neighboring books together to close the hole — you can see exactly where it used to sit.
- Pairs directly with Extract: if Lift is "take it out, leave a gap," Extract is "take it out, close the gap" — teaching them side by side as a matched pair usually lands better than teaching either alone. See [[Extract]].
