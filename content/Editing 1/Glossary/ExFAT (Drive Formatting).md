# ExFAT (Drive Formatting)

## Definition
A cross-platform disk format readable and writable by both macOS and Windows without extra drivers — the format of choice for a shared hard drive that needs to move between student machines running different operating systems.

## Teaching Analogies & Examples
- **A universal power adapter for international travel**: a drive formatted as ExFAT can plug into a Mac or a PC without needing a converter — the same reason a good travel adapter works in any country's outlet.
