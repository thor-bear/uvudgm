# M&E Track

## Definition
A version of a film's audio mix with dialogue removed entirely, but all music and sound effects intact — delivered so a foreign distributor can dub in a new dialogue track in another language without having to remix the whole film from scratch.

## Teaching Analogies & Examples
- **A recipe with the seasoning left in but the meat swapped out**: dialogue is like the protein of the mix — it's what gets replaced for a foreign release — but the music and sound effects ("seasoning") stay exactly as they were, so the dish still tastes like itself.
- Frame it as the practical payoff of good Foley: without it, an M&E track would have obvious silent holes anywhere a physical sound (footsteps, a door) only ever existed on the dialogue mic.
