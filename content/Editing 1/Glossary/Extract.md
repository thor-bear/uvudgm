# Extract

## Definition
Removes a selected clip (or the material between In/Out points) from the sequence and ripples everything after it earlier to close the resulting gap — unlike [[Lift]], the timeline stays contiguous.

## In Practice
Shortcut: **X**. Central to the **Radio Edit** workflow taught in Lesson 9 — the primary tool for cleaning up pauses, "errrs," and "uhhhs" from rough interview footage, demoed with prepared examples. Resurfaces as a recurring gap on review days: flagged explicitly in Lesson 13 as a concept students commonly still need reinforcement on even after Lesson 9, and reviewed again in Lesson 23's Premiere-side pass alongside Ripple/Roller trimming and mouse-free editing.

## Teaching Analogies & Examples
- **Pulling a book off the shelf and sliding everything over**: unlike Lift, Extract closes the gap immediately — the books on either side scoot together like nothing was ever missing.
- The "errrs and uhhhs" framing already used for the Radio Edit is itself a great in-class example: ask a student to imagine physically cutting a bad word out of an audio tape with scissors and taping the two ends back together — that's Extract.
- Paired teaching moment with Lift: "Lift leaves a hole, Extract closes it" is the one-line version worth repeating until it sticks. See [[Lift]].
