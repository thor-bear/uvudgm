# Nesting Effects

## Definition
Stacking multiple effects on a single clip so they apply in sequence, with the ability to rearrange their order in the Effects Editor and even swap out the underlying shot without breaking the effect chain built on top of it.

## In Practice
Covered in Lesson 27 as part of the Avid half of Effects Firehose day, right after the PIP Effect discussion — the lesson specifically shows rearranging nested effects and replacing a shot without disturbing the effects layered on it, reinforcing that an effect stack is portable and editable, not a fragile one-time build.

## Teaching Analogies & Examples
- **Stacking sheets of colored transparency film**: each effect is its own clear sheet layered on top of the last — you can reorder the stack, and you can slide a new photo underneath the whole stack without disturbing how the sheets are layered on top.
