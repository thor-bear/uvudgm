# Licensing Agreement

## Definition
The paperwork governing how footage licensed for educational/practice use (like the Jacuzzi short film, sourced through EditStock) can actually be used — what's allowed for classwork and portfolio reels versus what's required if a finished cut is ever posted publicly.

## Teaching Analogies & Examples
- **A gym membership vs. owning the gym**: the footage license lets you use the equipment (the material) to train — practice and build a reel — but it doesn't make the gym yours to sell or claim as your own production.
- The EditStock-logo-and-credit-only rule is worth stating as a simple two-part test: can I use this to practice and show my editing skill? Yes. Can I present it publicly as if I made the whole film? No.
