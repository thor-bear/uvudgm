# Fast Menu

## Definition
A small dropdown menu (denoted by a distinctive icon) attached to a specific Avid window — Bin, Timeline, Source/Record monitor — offering settings and view options scoped just to that window, rather than a global application menu.

## Teaching Analogies & Examples
- **The little gear icon on one specific app widget**: not the phone's main Settings app — just the tiny menu tucked into the corner of one particular window, controlling only that window's own options.
