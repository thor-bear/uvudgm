# Fast Menu

## Definition
A small dropdown menu (denoted by a distinctive icon) attached to a specific Avid window — Bin, Timeline, Source/Record monitor — offering settings and view options scoped just to that window, rather than a global application menu.

## In Practice
Used in Lesson 6 to switch the Sequence's Fast Menu to **Source Color** specifically so a clip's color label becomes visible directly in the timeline during the Splice-In demo — a small but necessary step, since color labels applied in a bin don't automatically display in the sequence without this toggle. Worth knowing simply as "the little menu on this specific window," since it recurs anywhere a per-window setting needs adjusting.

## Teaching Analogies & Examples
- **The little gear icon on one specific app widget**: not the phone's main Settings app — just the tiny menu tucked into the corner of one particular window, controlling only that window's own options.
