# Match Frame

## Definition
A command that jumps from a shot in the sequence back to the exact matching frame of that same shot in its Source/bin clip — instantly locating the original footage a sequence clip came from.

## Teaching Analogies & Examples
- **"Take me to where this came from"**: like clicking a thumbnail in a photo app and being taken straight to the full-size original in its folder — Match Frame does that for a shot in your sequence, jumping straight back to that exact frame in the source clip.
- Useful framing for students: it's the "undo the trip" button — you already made the edit, now you want to retrace your steps back to the raw material without hunting for it manually.
