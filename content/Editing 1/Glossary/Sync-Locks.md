# Sync-Locks

## Definition
A track-level lock that keeps a track's contents from being affected by ripple edits happening on other tracks — protects a track (most often music) from shifting out of place when picture and dialogue get trimmed around it.

## In Practice
Introduced in Lesson 6 right after laying the "We Are Young and We Are Free" music track onto the Montage sequence: the lesson pairs *discussing* sync-locks with the practical step of unselecting the music track so it's genuinely unaffected by further edits elsewhere in the sequence. A quiet but important habit — without it, later ripple trims on the picture side can silently drag the music out of alignment with the cut.

## Teaching Analogies & Examples
- **Bolting the furniture to the floor before renovating**: everything else in the room can get rearranged, but the bolted-down piece stays exactly where it is no matter what construction happens around it.
