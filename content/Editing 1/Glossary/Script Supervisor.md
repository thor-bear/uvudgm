# Script Supervisor

## Definition
The on-set role responsible for tracking continuity (matching props, wardrobe, actor positions, and action across takes and setups) and maintaining the [[Lined Script]] — the production's record of what coverage exists for each scene.

## Teaching Analogies & Examples
- **The set's continuity detective**: constantly checking which hand held the coffee cup, which way the door was left open, exactly where an actor was standing — small details that would otherwise create a jarring mismatch if two takes shot hours apart get cut together.
- Frame this role explicitly from the editor's chair: "this is the one person on set who's already thinking about *your* job" — the Script Supervisor's entire output exists to make the edit easier, which is a good way to make an on-set role feel relevant to students who'll mostly be sitting at a desk.
