# Cinema Verité (Observational Documentary)

## Definition
A documentary approach built on unobtrusive, patient observation of real life as it happens, rather than staged interviews or a heavily directed shooting plan — the camera waits long enough that subjects stop performing for it and simply live.

## Teaching Analogies & Examples
- **A fly on the wall, not a talk-show host**: the camera isn't asking questions or steering the moment — it's just present long enough that people forget it's there and start acting like themselves.
- Useful framing for the patience requirement: compare it to waiting quietly with a wildlife camera versus walking up and asking the animal to perform — verité only works if you're willing to wait out the awkward, uneventful stretches to get the real moment.
