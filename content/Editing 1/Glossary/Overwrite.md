# Overwrite

## Definition
An editing operation that lays a clip into the sequence starting at the playhead (or an In point), replacing — overwriting — whatever was already there for that duration, without shifting anything else in the timeline.

## Teaching Analogies & Examples
- **Think of a whiteboard**: Overwrite is like erasing just the section you're writing over and putting new text in its place — the rest of the board doesn't move.
- Contrast with Splice-In using a bookshelf: Overwrite takes a book off the shelf and puts a different one in the exact same spot; Splice-In shoves the whole shelf over to make room for a new book without removing anything.
## Related Terms
[[Splice-In]] — the insert-and-ripple counterpart to Overwrite. [[In & Out Points]] — Overwrite typically consumes whatever's marked between In and Out in the Source.
