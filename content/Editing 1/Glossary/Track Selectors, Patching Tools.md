# Track Selectors / Patching Tools

## Definition
The controls that decide which Source tracks feed which Record/Sequence tracks during an edit — "patching" a Source track to a Record track so, for example, audio channel 2 in the Source ends up on the right track in the sequence rather than being silently dropped or misplaced.

## In Practice
Introduced in Lesson 5's Princess Bride 5.1 demo, where green source-selection tracks are shown to be entirely about the Source clip and unrelated to the sequence — a point worth pausing on, since students commonly conflate the green (Source) and blue (Record/Sequence) selectors early on. Lesson 6 covers the mechanics directly: toggling patches via auto-patching, click-and-hold-select, or the string/arrow method; quick keys **7–0** for individual tracks; **Cmd+A** to select all, **Shift+Cmd+A** to deselect all; and Shift-drag with the mouse to toggle a range. Getting this wrong is a common, quiet source of "why is there no audio in my export" problems down the line (see [[Export to File]]'s "Use Only Active Tracks" caveat).

## Teaching Analogies & Examples
- **Plugging a cable into the right jack**: like patching a guitar into channel 2 of an amp instead of channel 1 — the signal only goes where it's physically connected, and Avid's track patching works the same way between Source and Record tracks.
- The green-vs-blue color distinction is the fastest memory hook: "green talks about the Source, blue talks about the Sequence" — repeat that pairing verbatim until it's automatic.
