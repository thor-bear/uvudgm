# YouTube Export ("Made for Kids" Flag)

## Definition
Premiere's ability to log directly into a connected YouTube channel and upload a finished export without leaving the app — followed by a required manual step on YouTube's side, setting whether the uploaded content is designated as made for children.

## In Practice
Demonstrated in Lesson 22 as the closing piece of the Export section, after covering Export basics (range settings, excluding audio, etc.). The lesson flags the "made for kids" designation specifically as easy to forget: students tend to assume the job is done the moment the file finishes transferring, missing that YouTube still requires this follow-up classification before the video is fully live and compliant.

## Teaching Analogies & Examples
- **Shipping a package that still needs a customs form filled out**: the file transfer completing is like the package arriving at the border — it's not actually cleared and released until the extra paperwork (the made-for-kids designation) gets filled out on YouTube's side.
