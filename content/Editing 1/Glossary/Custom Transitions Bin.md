# Custom Transitions Bin

## Definition
A bin named specifically **Custom Transitions** — creating one causes its contents to automatically surface inside the Effects Palette, giving a project-wide shortcut to any transition template saved there.

## Teaching Analogies & Examples
- **A magic folder name**: like how some apps auto-sync anything dropped into a specific folder — name the bin exactly "Custom Transitions" and Avid automatically makes its contents available in the Effects Palette, no extra setup step required.
