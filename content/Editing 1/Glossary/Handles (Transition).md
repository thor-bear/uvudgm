# Handles (Transition)

## Definition
The extra frames of media beyond a clip's used In/Out points — material that exists on disk but isn't currently visible in the cut. A transition like a dissolve needs handle on both sides of the edit to draw from, since it blends frames from the outgoing and incoming shots that wouldn't otherwise be shown.

## Teaching Analogies & Examples
- **Extra fabric left in a hem**: a tailor leaves spare material folded under so a garment can be let out later — Handles are that same spare, unused footage sitting just past your In/Out points, ready to be pulled from if a transition needs it.
