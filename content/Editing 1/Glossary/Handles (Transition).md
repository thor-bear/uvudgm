# Handles (Transition)

## Definition
The extra frames of media beyond a clip's used In/Out points — material that exists on disk but isn't currently visible in the cut. A transition like a dissolve needs handle on both sides of the edit to draw from, since it blends frames from the outgoing and incoming shots that wouldn't otherwise be shown.

## In Practice
Introduced in Lesson 7 directly out of the six-window display discussion: a dissolve needs at least half its total length available as handle on *both* sides of the cut, or the transition simply can't be built to full length. A foundational concept for understanding why a transition sometimes can't be applied, or gets silently shortened — not a bug, but a handle shortage.

## Teaching Analogies & Examples
- **Extra fabric left in a hem**: a tailor leaves spare material folded under so a garment can be let out later — Handles are that same spare, unused footage sitting just past your In/Out points, ready to be pulled from if a transition needs it.
- The "why can't I make this dissolve longer" moment is a great live-teaching opportunity: when a transition won't extend past a certain length, it's not a bug — the handle has simply run out, and this is the moment to show them why.
