# Slip

## Definition
A trim that shifts which frames of the *source* media a clip displays, without changing the clip's length or position in the sequence — the in and out points move together, revealing earlier or later material from the same shot.

## Teaching Analogies & Examples
- **Sliding a picture frame's mat left and right**: the window itself (the clip's position and length in the sequence) doesn't move at all — you're just changing which part of the photo behind it shows through.
- Good contrast to set up early: "Slip changes *what* you see without moving *where* you see it" — versus Slide, which is the reverse.
