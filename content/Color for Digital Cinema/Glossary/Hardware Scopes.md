# Hardware Scopes

## Definition
Physical devices that read the actual SDI signal coming out of a DeckLink/UltraStudio, rather than relying on Resolve's internal software scopes.

## In Practice
More accurate in principle, but explicitly flagged in this course as expensive and likely unnecessary for most students, given how much Resolve's built-in scopes have improved. Named examples: **Omni Scope** (used on RED cameras) and **Colorfront** (used by major studios like Disney/Netflix/Warner Bros., priced high enough that it's not publicly listed).

## Teaching Analogies & Examples
- **A multimeter vs. trusting the device's own readout**: a phone charger might display "80% charged" on screen, but a multimeter reading the actual current tells you what's really happening electrically, independent of whatever the device chooses to report. A hardware scope reads the real SDI signal the same way — it doesn't trust Resolve's own internal math, it measures the wire directly.
