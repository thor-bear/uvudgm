# 3D Keyer

## Definition
A different selection algorithm from the standard [[HSL Qualifier]] — sometimes performs better, sometimes worse, shot-dependent. Worth trying both when a selection is being stubborn.

## In Practice
Has a built-in [[Despill]] tool, useful specifically for actual green-screen work. This course is candid that the exact internal difference between the 3D Keyer and the HSL Qualifier isn't fully understood even by the instructor — worth trying both on a difficult selection rather than assuming one is always superior.

## Teaching Analogies & Examples
- **A different lock-picking technique for the same door**: the HSL Qualifier and the 3D Keyer both aim to select the same thing (a range of color) but use different internal algorithms to get there — sometimes one picks the lock cleanly where the other struggles, and it's worth trying the second one when the first is producing a chattery or incomplete selection, even without fully understanding why the difference exists (a fair thing to admit, since this course's own instructor did too).
