# P3-DCI

## Definition
The theatrical color space — wider gamut than Rec. 709. Traditionally SDR in cinema use (gamma 2.6), though the same P3 gamut paired with a different gamma (e.g. Netflix/Apple TV's preferred PQ gamma) becomes HDR. Also used on iMac 5K/iPhone/iPad displays.

## In Practice
Most "Rec. 2020" HDR content is actually mastered in P3's gamut paired with a PQ gamma, since almost no consumer display can reproduce true Rec. 2020 — see [[Rec. 2020]]. DCP deliverables (see [[DCP (Digital Cinema Package)]]) ultimately convert into DCI-P3 for theatrical projection regardless of the working color space used during grading.

## Teaching Analogies & Examples
- **The movie-theater-specific box of crayons**: P3-DCI is a wider, more saturated color space than Rec. 709, built specifically for digital cinema projection — think of it as the palette a theater's projector is capable of showing, distinct from the palette a home TV is built around (Rec. 709/2020).
