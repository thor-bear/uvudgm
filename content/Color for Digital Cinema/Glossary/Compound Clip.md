# Compound Clip

## Definition
Multiple clips nested and treated as one manageable unit on the timeline (select clips, right-click → New Compound Clip).

## In Practice
Recommended best practice in this course once a composite (like a [[Efforts/Notes/Courses/Color for Digital Cinema/Glossary/Sky Replacement]]) is finished — keeps the timeline tidy rather than leaving multiple separately-graded layers exposed. Not to be confused with a [[Atlas/Notes/Student Notes/Color for Digital Cinema/Glossary/Compound Node]] (a node-level, not clip-level, nesting).

## Teaching Analogies & Examples
- **Zipping several files into one archive**: a Compound Clip bundles multiple clips/layers on a timeline into one single clip you can move, trim, or grade as a unit — the individual pieces are still inside, just tucked behind one wrapper, the same underlying idea as a [[Atlas/Notes/Student Notes/Color for Digital Cinema/Glossary/Compound Node]] but applied to timeline clips instead of a node tree.
