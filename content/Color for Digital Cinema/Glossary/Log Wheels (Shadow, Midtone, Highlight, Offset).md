# Log Wheels (Shadow, Midtone, Highlight, Offset)

## Definition
Wheels labeled Shadow/Midtone/Highlight/Offset — the same conceptual controls as [[Atlas/Notes/Student Notes/Color for Digital Cinema/Glossary/Primary Wheels (Lift, Gamma, Gain, Offset)]], relabeled and designed to operate pre-color-space-conversion on log data.

## In Practice
More surgically targeted than the primary wheels — adjusting "shadow" barely touches midtones visibly — but with a much harsher cutoff between zones, meaning small adjustments can break an image fast. Best used for narrow, precise range targeting (a manually adjustable range slider lets you redefine what counts as "shadow," etc.) rather than general grading. The course's general recommendation: stick with primary lift/gamma/gain for most work and only reach for log/HDR wheels when a shot genuinely needs a narrow, surgical adjustment.

## Teaching Analogies & Examples
- **Lift/Gamma/Gain's cousin, tuned for log material**: same basic idea — three (or four, with Offset) zone-based wheels — but calibrated to behave sensibly on log-encoded footage's compressed, flattened range, where a standard Lift/Gamma/Gain adjustment can feel disproportionate or oddly weighted.
