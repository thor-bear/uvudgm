# Hue

## Definition
The "color" part of color — a position on the color wheel, independent of brightness or intensity. Demonstrated by keying an object and rotating the hue slider through the full wheel (cyan → green → yellow → red/pink) without changing brightness.

## In Practice
Central to nearly every keying and curve tool in this course — the HSL Qualifier keys by hue (among other things), and the Hue-vs-Hue, Hue-vs-Sat, and Hue-vs-Lum curves each use hue as the *selection* axis. Distinct from [[Saturation]] (intensity of a hue) — the two are frequently adjusted together but are independent properties.

## Teaching Analogies & Examples
- **The position on the color wheel, full stop**: Hue is just "which color" — red, orange, blue-green — with no information about how bright or intense it is. It's the one axis that stays fixed while Saturation (intensity) and Luminance (brightness) vary independently around it, which is exactly what the Vectorscope's circular layout is designed to show.
