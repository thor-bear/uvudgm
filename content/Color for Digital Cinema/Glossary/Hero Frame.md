# Hero Frame

## Definition
The frame chosen as the starting point for drawing a shape or beginning a track — not necessarily frame 1 of the shot, but wherever the subject is most fully and clearly in view, giving the tracker (and the shape-drawing itself) the best starting data.

## In Practice
A simple but easy-to-skip habit: before tracking or keyframing a power window, scrub to find the shot's best-visibility frame rather than defaulting to the first frame, since a poor starting frame produces a worse initial selection that both tracking and keyframing then have to compensate for.

## Teaching Analogies & Examples
- **Picking the best single photo from a burst before editing the whole sequence**: rather than tracking or keyframing a window across every single frame of a moving shot, you find the one frame that best represents the shot's key moment, build the grade there, and let that "hero" frame's setup carry (or get tracked) across the rest.
