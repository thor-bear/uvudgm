# Bypass vs. Disable (Grade/Node Toggles)

## Definition
Three related but distinct toggle shortcuts for temporarily turning off grading work: **Bypass entire grade** (Shift+D, a non-destructive preview toggle for the whole grade), **Disable all nodes** (Option+D), and **Disable/toggle just the selected node** (Command+D).

## In Practice
Easily confused since all three produce a similar "turn something off" effect at different scopes — worth knowing which one is being used when troubleshooting why a shot looks ungraded, or when comparing before/after states quickly without building a full new [[Versions (Grade Versioning)|Version]].

## Teaching Analogies & Examples
- **A light switch vs. unplugging the lamp**: Disable is the light switch — flip it back on and everything's instantly as it was. Bypass (of the whole grade) is closer to briefly unplugging to check what the room looks like with no lamp at all — a quick global "what did this shot look like before I touched it" check.
- Good habit to state directly: reach for Bypass when you want to see the raw, ungraded image for comparison; reach for node-level Disable when you're isolating which specific node is responsible for a problem you're troubleshooting.
