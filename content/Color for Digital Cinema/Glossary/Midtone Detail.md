# Midtone Detail

## Definition
Increases contrast specifically within the midtone range rather than globally — produces a perceived-sharpness boost without literally increasing edge sharpness the way a Sharpen tool does.

## In Practice
Useful for adding "grit" to an image without over-sharpening actual edges. Warned against overuse in this course — pushed too far it reads as artificial/over-processed. One of several building blocks of "the film look" alongside grain and sharpness/softness — see [[Look (Color Grade)]].

## Teaching Analogies & Examples
- **Turning up "texture" without turning up "sharpness"**: Sharpen boosts edge contrast specifically (the literal outlines get crisper); Midtone Detail instead boosts contrast within the broad middle range of tones, producing a grittier, more textured feel without the telltale crunchy edges a real sharpen tool leaves behind. Worth demonstrating both side by side so the difference is visible, not just described.
