# Rec. 2020

## Definition
A much wider gamut than Rec. 709 or P3-DCI, paired with the ST.2084/PQ gamma for HDR delivery (Netflix, Amazon, 4K UHD Blu-ray). So large that almost no consumer screens can reproduce its full gamut yet — most content labeled "Rec. 2020" is actually mastered in P3 gamut with a PQ gamma, anticipating displays catching up. A handful of extremely expensive reference monitors (Dolby Pulsar, Sony BVM-HX310) can approach true Rec. 2020 reproduction.

## In Practice
Color spaces even wider than Rec. 2020 exist for archival mastering — intentionally oversized "sandboxes" trimmed down for actual display later. Comes up in this course primarily in the HDR lesson alongside PQ and Dolby Vision — see [[Atlas/Notes/Student Notes/Color for Digital Cinema/Glossary/HDR Delivery Formats (Dolby Vision, HDR10, HDR10+)]].

## Teaching Analogies & Examples
- **The 4K-TV-era crayon box**: Rec. 2020 is the wide-gamut standard built for modern UHD/HDR displays — a noticeably bigger box of crayons than Rec. 709's older, narrower set, matching how much further modern displays can actually push color and brightness compared to the broadcast TVs Rec. 709 was designed around.
