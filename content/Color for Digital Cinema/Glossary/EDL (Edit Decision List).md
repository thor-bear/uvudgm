# EDL (Edit Decision List)

## Definition
One of the interchange formats an NLE can export a locked timeline as, alongside [[Atlas/Notes/Student Notes/Color for Digital Cinema/Glossary/XML (Editing Interchange)]] and [[Atlas/Notes/Student Notes/Color for Digital Cinema/Glossary/AAF (Advanced Authoring Format)]], for automatic [[Atlas/Notes/Student Notes/Color for Digital Cinema/Glossary/Conforming]] into another application.

## In Practice
Mentioned in this course as one of several export options — a simpler, older interchange format than XML or AAF, generally less feature-rich (limited effects/metadata support) but broadly compatible.

## Teaching Analogies & Examples
- **The oldest, plainest interchange format still in daily use — like a fax that still works when nothing fancier will go through**: an EDL is a simple, text-based list of cuts and timecodes with none of the richness AAF or XML carry (no effects, no nested sequences) — old technology, but still reliably readable by nearly everything, which is exactly why it survives as a fallback option.
