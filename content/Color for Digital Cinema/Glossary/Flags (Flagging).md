# Flags (Flagging)

## Definition
Assigning a color flag to clips sharing an angle/setup (e.g. all wide shots flagged green) via the Clips dropdown → Flag. Clips can then be filtered to show only a specific flag color, letting a colorist grade by *setup* rather than linearly through the timeline.

## In Practice
If the same source file is used multiple times in the edit, flagging one instance automatically flags all instances. The foundational first step of the [[Atlas/Notes/Student Notes/Color for Digital Cinema/Glossary/Group Grading (Groups)]] workflow: flag by setup, filter to just that flag, select all, then add to a new group.

## Teaching Analogies & Examples
- **Sticky flags on a stack of paperwork**: a quick colored marker on a clip meaning "come back to this," "problem shot," or "approved" — lightweight, visual, and meant to be scanned at a glance across a whole timeline, the same purpose as physical page flags in a stack of documents.
