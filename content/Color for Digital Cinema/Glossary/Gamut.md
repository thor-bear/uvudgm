# Gamut

## Definition
The spectrum of colors available within a color space — its "width." A wider gamut (a bigger triangle on the color-space horseshoe diagram) can reproduce more distinct colors. Often confused with gamma, but the two are independent: gamut is about which hues exist, gamma is about brightness/contrast.

## In Practice
Rec. 709 has the narrowest gamut of the three spaces compared in this course; P3-DCI is wider (theatrical); Rec. 2020 is wider still (HDR delivery), so wide that almost no consumer display can reproduce it fully yet — most "Rec. 2020" content is actually mastered in P3 gamut with a PQ gamma curve, anticipating displays catching up.

## Teaching Analogies & Examples
- **The fenced yard a dog can actually run in**: Gamut is the boundary of every color a given system can represent — anything outside the fence simply isn't reachable, no matter how hard you push a slider. A wider gamut (Rec. 2020) is a bigger yard; a narrower one (Rec. 709) is a smaller yard with the same dog inside it.
