# Triadic (Color Scheme)

## Definition
Three dominant colors forming a triangle on the color wheel.

## In Practice
Superman's red/blue/yellow costume/branding is cited in this course as the most iconic example — functions similarly to a complementary scheme in making elements pop via clear color distinction, just with three anchor colors instead of two.

## Teaching Analogies & Examples
- **A three-legged stool instead of a two-person tug-of-war**: Complementary is two colors pulling against each other; Triadic spreads that same "make things pop through contrast" idea across three anchor colors forming a triangle on the wheel instead of a straight line. Superman's red/blue/yellow branding is the go-to reference — instantly recognizable, and a clean three-point example students already know without needing a film clip.
