# HDR Wheels

## Definition
The most granular of the three wheel sets covered in this course — blacks / darks / shadows / midtones / lights / highlights / speculars. The modern descendant of the [[Primary Wheels (Lift, Gamma, Gain, Offset)]] and [[Log Wheels (Shadow, Midtone, Highlight, Offset)]].

## In Practice
Powerful but can get overwhelming with that many zones — this course's explicit recommendation is to stick with primary lift/gamma/gain for most work and only reach for HDR wheels (or log wheels) when a shot genuinely needs a surgical, narrow-range adjustment. Noted as working better than the standard Temperature slider specifically within color-managed workflows for certain corrections.

## Teaching Analogies & Examples
- **Wheels rebuilt for a much bigger dynamic range**: standard Lift/Gamma/Gain wheels were designed with SDR's more limited brightness range in mind — HDR Wheels are the same basic idea reworked to give useful, proportional control across HDR's much wider range, so a small nudge doesn't accidentally represent a huge jump in actual brightness.
