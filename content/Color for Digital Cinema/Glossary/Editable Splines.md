# Editable Splines

## Definition
A toggle (three-dot menu → Editable Splines) on Resolve's custom curves that enables more granular, direct control over the curve's shape — used for adding midtone texture/contrast beyond what the default curve points allow.

## In Practice
Used in this course's look-building/practicing-looks work to shape specific tonal regions precisely — for example, bringing highlights down to a target parade value to match a reference film's dark highlight ceiling, or adding subtle midtone texture as part of a color-scheme push.

## Teaching Analogies & Examples
- **Bending a flexible ruler instead of connecting dots with straight lines**: a spline curve doesn't just connect its control points with straight segments — it bends smoothly through them, which is why a curve grade reads as gradual and organic rather than jointed and mechanical.
