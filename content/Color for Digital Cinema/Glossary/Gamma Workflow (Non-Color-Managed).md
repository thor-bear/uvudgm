# Gamma Workflow (Non-Color-Managed)

## Definition
The simpler, non-color-managed grading approach used as this course's default through most of the semester: take footage as-is, adjust it by eye until it looks right on screen, done. The grade is permanently limited to whatever display color space it was graded in — no clean path to later deliver P3, HDR, or another spec without starting over.

## In Practice
Contrasted throughout the course against [[Atlas/Notes/Student Notes/Color for Digital Cinema/Glossary/Color-Managed Workflow]] via two recurring metaphors: legroom on an airplane (cramped gamma workflow vs. stretched-out color-managed workflow, even landing in the same seat/delivery format eventually), and a bigger box of crayons (12-pack vs. 100-pack — same idea, more room to work regardless of final output). This course's default project settings all semester (DaVinci YRGB, Timeline/Output Rec. 709 Gamma 2.4) are a gamma workflow.

## Teaching Analogies & Examples
- **Driving stick shift**: this is the "manual transmission" side of the Color-Managed Workflow analogy — you personally decide the timeline color space and handle conversions with tools like CST, rather than letting an automated pipeline shift gears for you. Not worse, just more hands-on, and exactly why this class starts here (DaVinci YRGB) before introducing the automatic version later.
