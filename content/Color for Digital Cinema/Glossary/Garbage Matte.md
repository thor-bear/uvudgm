# Garbage Matte

## Definition
A rough shape around a subject, softened, deliberately never graded directly — used only as a selection to duplicate downstream into other nodes.

## In Practice
Built on its own node (labeled clearly, e.g. "Face Power Window"), then its selection is copied into fresh nodes by dragging the node's blue output into a new node's blue input — this copies the *selection*, not any grading, letting multiple independent corrections all pull from the same master key. For a two-subject shot, the second person's garbage matte is often built as an [[Outside Node]] of the first person's shape rather than drawn separately, guaranteeing the two selections are perfect complements with no gaps or overlaps.

## Teaching Analogies & Examples
- **A rough first pass with scissors before the fine detail work**: a Garbage Matte is a loose, imprecise shape that just throws out the obviously-irrelevant parts of the frame — it doesn't need to be pixel-accurate, just good enough to give a more precise key or selection a much smaller, easier area to work within afterward.
