# Output Blanking

## Definition
Visual crop guides shown while grading, for the colorist's own reference — not something that gets baked into the delivered image.

## In Practice
A critical export gotcha: turn Output Blanking off before exporting a graded master. Leaving it on bakes black bars into the delivered clips instead of exporting the full open-gate frame for the online editor to crop as needed.

## Teaching Analogies & Examples
- **Matting a picture frame to hide the raw edges**: Output Blanking masks off a defined border around the outside of the frame, hiding any unwanted edge artifacts (dead pixels at the sensor's border, cropping irregularities) the same way a picture-frame mat hides a photo's rough physical edge without cropping the actual printed image underneath.
