# Outside Node

## Definition
Selects the *inverse* of whatever selection exists on the node it's attached to (Option+O). Requires a prior selection to invert — on an unselected node it has nothing to invert and does nothing.

## In Practice
The other half of nearly every eye-direction demo in this course: window/key the subject, brighten it, then add an outside node to darken/desaturate everything else. Also used to build a two-subject garbage matte efficiently (build one person's shape, make the second person an outside node of it, guaranteeing the two selections are perfect complements with no gaps or overlaps) — see [[Atlas/Notes/Student Notes/Color for Digital Cinema/Glossary/Garbage Matte]]. Combines naturally with [[Atlas/Notes/Student Notes/Color for Digital Cinema/Glossary/Power Windows]] to grab the inverse of whatever the window selected.

## Teaching Analogies & Examples
- **The photo negative of a selection**: if a power window or key selects "this," an Outside Node grabs everything that is *not* "this" — like a stencil where you're painting the area around the cutout instead of through it. A common pairing: window the subject with one node, grab everything else with an Outside Node to grade the background separately without re-selecting anything.
