# Histogram

## Definition
Also reads luminance, left-to-right, similar in principle to the [[Waveform (Scope)]].

## In Practice
Explicitly dismissed in this course as mostly redundant with Parade/Waveform and less useful overall — the one concession being it shows slightly more precision near the shadow end. Not a tool actively recommended for leaning on; flagged alongside log/HDR wheels as something students can safely deprioritize.

## Teaching Analogies & Examples
- **A population chart, not a map**: a Histogram shows *how many pixels* exist at each brightness level, stacked up like a bar chart — a tall spike on the left means a lot of the image is dark, but unlike the Waveform, it tells you nothing about *where* in the frame those dark pixels actually are.
