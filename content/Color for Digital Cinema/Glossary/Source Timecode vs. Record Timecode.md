# Source Timecode vs. Record Timecode

## Definition
Two easily-confused values: **source timecode** is a clip's own embedded timecode; **record timecode** is the timeline's. Always burn in **source**, not record, when adding metadata overlays to dailies.

## In Practice
Comes up specifically in the [[Atlas/Notes/Student Notes/Color for Digital Cinema/Glossary/Data Burn-In]] setup, where the correct choice (Source Clip Name, Source Timecode) is one of several settings whose failure mode is silent and hard to notice until it's a real problem downstream.

## Teaching Analogies & Examples
- **A book's internal page numbers vs. where it sits on the shelf**: Source Timecode is a clip's own internal clock, counting from wherever the camera started rolling that take. Record Timecode is where that same clip sits within the overall sequence timeline — like the difference between "page 47 of this book" and "book #3 on shelf position 12." Both describe the same clip, answering different questions.
