# MediaInfo

## Definition
A free diagnostic app (or ~$1 on the Mac App Store) for inspecting a file's actual codec, container, bitrate, bit depth, and audio-stream details — outside of Resolve.

## In Practice
Recommended in this course specifically because a file's container extension (.MOV, .MXF, etc.) tells you almost nothing about its actual codec or quality — see [[Atlas/Notes/Student Notes/Color for Digital Cinema/Glossary/Container (Wrapper)]]. Within Resolve itself, enabling the relevant metadata columns on the Media page shows Format (container) and Codec per clip directly, as a lighter-weight alternative to a separate app.

## Teaching Analogies & Examples
- **Reading the nutrition label on food you didn't buy yourself**: when a file's exact codec, container, or resolution is unclear just from opening it, MediaInfo reads the actual technical metadata directly and tells you precisely what's inside — no guessing from the filename or how it looks on screen.
