# Handles

## Definition
Frames that extend beyond a clip's edit points in the locked cut — relevant when delivering individual graded clips back to an editor (e.g. "send me clips with 12-frame handles") so they retain room to nudge/trim after the color pass without needing to re-render.

## In Practice
Set via Export → Advanced Settings → Handles (requires an XML-based export preset) — e.g. 24-frame handles on 24fps footage gives a full second of extra material on each end of every clip. Good practice on essentially all VFX-bound and DIT-stage exports, reinforced across multiple lessons in this course (initial DIT handoff, later VFX handoff before final grading).

## Teaching Analogies & Examples
- **Extra fabric folded under a hem, in the delivery/editorial sense**: the same "spare material left just past the edge" idea as Handles (Transition)'s transition handles, applied here to whole delivered clips — extra frames beyond the locked cut's edit points, so an editor receiving graded clips back can still nudge or trim slightly without needing a re-render from the colorist.
