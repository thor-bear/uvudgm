# Timeline Markers

## Definition
Markers placed on the Color-page timeline, used for two distinct purposes in this course: **team communication** (a color-coded meaning agreed upon within a team — e.g. red = "problem clip, needs discussion" — with a note including initials and date) and **personal progress tracking** (a marker indicating "where I left off"). Can extend across a range (in/out points), not just a single frame, and support named titles plus free-text notes.

## In Practice
Distinct from **marked clips**, a separate Edit-page-level marking system used for a different purpose (sorting/filtering clips in the media list) — the two systems are not interchangeable, a real point of confusion clarified via student Q&A in this course.

## Teaching Analogies & Examples
- **A sticky note on the timeline itself, not on any one clip**: unlike Flags (which live on a clip), Timeline Markers sit at a specific point in the overall sequence — good for noting a global concern ("client wants this section reviewed," "sync issue somewhere in here") that isn't really about any single shot.
