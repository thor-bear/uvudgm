# Despill

## Definition
A tool built into the [[3D Keyer]], used specifically for removing green-screen spill (unwanted green color cast bleeding onto a subject from a green-screen background).

## In Practice
Comes up in this course primarily as a name-check within the 3D Keyer discussion rather than a deeply demonstrated standalone technique — genuinely useful for real green-screen production work.

## Teaching Analogies & Examples
- **Washing green paint splatter off a subject standing too close to a green wall**: a green screen's light physically bounces onto whatever's in front of it, tinting edges (hair, skin) with a faint green cast. Despill specifically targets and removes that spill color without affecting the rest of the image's actual color — cleanup after the key, not part of making the selection itself.
