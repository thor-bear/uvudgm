# TRT (Total Running Time)

## Definition
The actual duration of a film's content, excluding the slate, two-pop, and leading black — but by convention still including any trailing black buffer added at the very end of the physical master file.

## In Practice
Calculated by setting an in-point at 01:00:00:00 (content start) and an out-point at the very last frame of actual content — navigate to End, then step back one frame before setting the out-point, since setting it directly at End over-counts by one frame. A genuinely fiddly, easy-to-get-wrong step; once known, typed into the [[Slate (Delivery)]] graphic.

## Teaching Analogies & Examples
- **The number printed on a movie ticket stub**: simply the film's exact final length, start to finish — a plain fact that still needs to be stated precisely and correctly in delivery paperwork, since scheduling, broadcast slots, and distribution logistics all depend on it being exact, not approximate.
