# Waveform (Scope)

## Definition
Shows luminance from top (brightest) to bottom (darkest); middle gray sits around the 40% mark.

## In Practice
Generally superseded by [[Parade (Scope)]] for actual grading work, since Parade shows the same luminance information split into three independent R/G/B traces — more useful for spotting channel-specific imbalances. Still the go-to for pure contrast/exposure decisions (e.g. watching that shadows don't crush below zero while brightening a selection).

## Teaching Analogies & Examples
- **A skyline silhouette of your image's brightness**: the Waveform plots luminance across the frame left-to-right, so a shot with a bright sky and dark foreground produces a tall peak up top and a low band down below — you're reading the shape of the image's brightness the way you'd read a city skyline's shape.
- Useful pairing to state directly: Waveform reads brightness, Parade reads brightness *per color channel*, Vectorscope reads hue/saturation with brightness stripped out entirely — three different questions about the same image, not three versions of the same scope.
