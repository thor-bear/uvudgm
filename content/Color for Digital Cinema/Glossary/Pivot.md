# Pivot

## Definition
The center point where the software divides "highlight" from "shadow" when applying [[Contrast]].

## In Practice
A personal technique shared in this course: nudging pivot slightly clockwise/brighter than default (which sits a bit into the shadow side) tends to give more pleasing results — offered explicitly as a preference, not a hard rule, and shot-dependent.

## Teaching Analogies & Examples
- **The fulcrum of a see-saw**: Contrast is how far the two ends of the see-saw tilt apart; Pivot is where the see-saw's center point sits. Move the Pivot and you change which tones stay put while Contrast stretches everything else around it — a Pivot set higher keeps highlights more fixed while shadows do more of the moving, and vice versa.
